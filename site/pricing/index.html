Live on XRPL Mainnet · priced per desk, not per query

Two questions. One ledger read. One answer.

Compliance tooling reads account flags and never opens the order book. Market terminals read the book and never look at who can freeze the balance. A desk holding an issued asset has to answer both — am I allowed to move this, and could I actually get out of it — and answering them from two systems at two moments produces a position that looks fine in each and is not. NOSHASHI reads both sides of the same validated ledger state, in the same second, and reconciles them into one verdict.

All plans read XRPL Mainnet. There is no testnet mode, because a compliance answer about a test network is not an answer.
