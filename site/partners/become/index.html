Become a partner

Carry ledger facts to your users, with us.

NOSHASHI is a read-only intelligence tool for the XRP Ledger. Partners put its readings where decisions are made: on token pages, in client files, inside wallets and in compliance stacks. Tell us what you run, and we will work out the simplest way to connect it.

Apply to partner See how partners use it

01 · INTEGRATION PARTNERS

Explorers, DEXes, launchpads and wallets

Show issuer controls next to every token, or check a destination before a user sends. The Asset Risk API and the live badge are free and need no key.

02 · REFERRAL PARTNERS

Auditors, accountants, law firms and advisers

Refer clients who hold XRPL assets, or order Ledger Evidence Reports for several clients at once. A referral fee or volume pricing is agreed in writing with each firm.

03 · CHANNEL PARTNERS

Consultancies, integrators and compliance vendors

Bring the workspace and its receipts to banks, custodians and treasuries you already serve, with terms set out per partner before anything is sold.

04 · DATA AND TECHNOLOGY PARTNERS

Node operators, analytics and custody technology

Use the same reads in your own product through the SDK and Compliance API, with every answer carrying the ledger index it was read at.

WHAT PARTNERS GET

  • Keyless access to issuer controls and the live badge from day one
  • A direct line to the founder for integration questions
  • Early access to new readings before they reach the public tools
  • A listing on the partners page once your integration is live, if you want one

WHAT WE ASK

  • A visible "Issuer facts by NOSHASHI" link wherever the readings appear
  • Show facts as facts: with the ledger they were read at, never as a score or a rating
  • Never present NOSHASHI as endorsing a token, an issuer or an investment

How it works: three steps, all remote

I.Tell us what you run. A short note through the contact form: your product, your users, and where ledger facts would help them.

II.A 30-minute call. We look at your flow together and pick the lightest integration that works.

III.Go live. Any commercial terms are written down first; then you connect, and we check the readings render the way the ledger reports them.
