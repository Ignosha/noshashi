Put ledger facts where your users already look.

NOSHASHI reads what XRP Ledger issuers can still do: freeze, claw back, gate holding, sign alone, and how much of a book can really fill. If your users pick tokens, hold them, or need to prove what they hold, you can give them those facts under your own roof.

EXPLORERS · DEXES · LAUNCHPADS

An issuer-controls column on every token page

Free, no key: https://www.noshashi.app/api/authority?issuer=r… returns the reading as JSON, and https://www.noshashi.app/api/badge?issuer=r… returns a live badge you can drop into a page as an image. Both are cached at the edge for five minutes and open to any origin. We ask for a visible "Issuer facts by NOSHASHI" link where they appear.

Launchpads can offer the Issuer check to projects at listing, so holders see the same facts the platform did.

AUDITORS · ACCOUNTANTS · LAW FIRMS

Ledger evidence for your clients' XRPL positions

A Ledger Evidence Report records what the ledger shows for the addresses a client names, at the date you name: issuer controls, holdings and who can move them, and real against advertised depth, with a fingerprint anyone can check. It is a record of facts, not an opinion, so it sits alongside your own work rather than replacing it.

Firms that refer clients, or order for several clients, get terms agreed in writing: a referral fee or volume pricing, invoice or bank transfer.

Check an address before your user sends

The address check runs as a mobile page: sanctions list, scam registry and the ledger's own flags for the destination, with nothing signed. It is built to open inside Xaman as an xApp, and any wallet can open it with ?address=r….

COMMUNITIES

Bots for Telegram and Discord

Send /check r… to read what an issuer can do right now, or /watch r… to be told the moment it changes: freeze, clawback, signing keys, fees. Discord channels can also get change alerts through a webhook. Free for three watched issuers per chat.

WHAT NOSHASHI NEVER DOES

It never holds keys, never signs, and never asks anyone for a seed, a key or a balance. Every fact it shows is read from validated ledger state and names the ledger it was read at. A fact it could not read is shown as not read, never as a pass. There is no score and no verdict on a token.

Developers: the same reads are in the SDK and Compliance API. Source: GitHub.
