Scope & limits

No shell execution, keys, signing, uploads, saved cases or customer-data storage. Commands and history stay in memory until reload. Queries reach NOSHASHI and public XRPL nodes; platform logs may record requests.

Six requests/minute per caller per warm instance; bounded provider and concurrency budgets also apply. These are demo controls, not a global service guarantee.

Issuer permissions do not prove solvency, reserves or legal rights. Node reads do not independently verify consensus or compliance. A hash is not a signature.
