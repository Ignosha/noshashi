Why this is here

Context, kept separate from signal

News is not a verdict

A story about an issuer is not a reading of that issuer's freeze rights, and a rally is not liquidity you could exit into. The product answers those from the ledger. This page exists so the two are in the same place without ever being the same thing.

How it is assembled

Google News, Cointelegraph's XRP tag and CoinDesk, read server-side. Titles are de-duplicated across sources, sorted by publication time, and linked back to the publisher. No article text is copied and no source is rewritten.
