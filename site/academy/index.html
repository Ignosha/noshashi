01 / ISSUER CONTROLS

Who holds the controls?

Read freeze, clawback and authorization settings. Learn what those flags can establish, and what they cannot prove about reserves or legal rights.

02 / PAYMENT EVIDENCE

What was delivered?

Examine the delivered amount, understand partial payments, and recognize when the evidence is incomplete.

03 / FUNDED DEPTH

What depth is funded?

Distinguish quoted offers from funded order-book depth. A snapshot is evidence at a point in time, not an execution guarantee.
