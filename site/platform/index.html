How it works

From ledger data to verifiable evidence.

  1. 01 · READ

    Read the ledger

    Inspect the relevant XRPL data from validated ledgers.

  2. 02 · ANALYZE

    Run the checks

    Issuer controls, delivered amounts, fillable depth and account settings.

  3. 03 · VERIFY

    See the evidence

    The result, the rule behind it and the ledger it was read at.

  4. 04 · EXPORT

    Keep the proof

    A SHA-256 receipt and exportable evidence for review.
