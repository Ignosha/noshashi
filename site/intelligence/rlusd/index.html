      03 / ASK THE RIGHT QUESTION

      Evidence before interpretation.

      Supply

      Issuer obligations and contract supply describe outstanding token accounting. Mint/burn attribution needs verified transaction evidence and a defined interval.

      Inspect issuer permissions ↗

      Liquidity

      Funded offers and pool reserves describe particular venues at a moment in time. They do not guarantee an executable price.

      Inspect funded depth ↗

      Activity

      Transfers and trades are different events. No market-wide volume, holder concentration or historical mint/burn total has been measured here.

      Inspect delivered evidence ↗
