NOSHASHI runs without an account and, in that mode, without sending anything about you anywhere. Creating an account changes that, and this policy is explicit about exactly where the line falls.

What we do not collect

NOSHASHI Labs operates no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no advertising identifiers. We do not profile you and we do not sell or share data with advertisers.

Signing in is optional. Without an account, the console reads public ledger data and stores your preferences locally, and no request identifying you reaches any server we operate. This is the default and it is fully functional.

Public ledger data

TERMS OF USE

These terms govern your use of NOSHASHI. Read the disclaimers — they describe real limits on what this software can be relied upon for.

What this software is

NOSHASHI is a read-only compliance console for the XRP Ledger. It reads public ledger state, evaluates it against a configurable policy rule set, and returns an explainable verdict with a cryptographic receipt. It does not custody assets, hold keys, or sign or broadcast transactions.

Not advice

Nothing produced by this software — verdicts, receipts, agent responses, or the commercial plan shown in the console — is legal advice, regulatory advice, tax advice, or investment advice.

A GO verdict means the configured rules passed. It is not a representation that a transaction is lawful in your jurisdiction, and it does not discharge any obligation you owe to a regulator. A NO-GO verdict is not a determination that anyone has done anything wrong.

Compliance determinations that carry legal consequence must be reviewed by qualified counsel and the responsible compliance officer.

The rule set is yours

The domain registry and policy rules shipped with this build are reference definitions for demonstration. Before operational use you are responsible for configuring rules that reflect the obligations that actually apply to you, and for keeping them current as those obligations change.

REGULATORY DISCLOSURES

Where this software sits relative to financial regulation, stated plainly so nobody has to infer it.

Not a regulated financial institution

NOSHASHI Labs is not a bank, a broker-dealer, a money services business, a money transmitter, a qualified custodian, or a registered investment adviser. It does not hold client assets or client funds at any point.

Not a sanctions screening provider of record

The sanctions-related rules in this console evaluate credentials asserted on-ledger by third-party issuers. They are not a substitute for screening against an authoritative sanctions list by a provider of record, and they do not discharge any screening obligation you hold.

Credential issuers are third parties

A credential is only as reliable as the issuer that signed it. This software verifies that a credential exists on the ledger, is accepted by its subject, and has not been revoked. It cannot verify that the issuer performed adequate diligence before issuing it.

Fees and licensing

Any fee charged for compliance verification may attract licensing obligations depending on jurisdiction and on how the service is characterised. This has not yet been assessed by counsel and must be before any fee is charged in production.

Reporting a problem

Security vulnerabilities: security@noshashi.app. We ask for coordinated disclosure and will acknowledge within 1 business day. Regulatory or legal correspondence: legal@noshashi.app.
