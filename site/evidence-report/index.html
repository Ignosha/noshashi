Ledger Evidence Report

For a company that holds XRP or issues on the XRP Ledger, and has to show an auditor, a board or a counterparty what the ledger says. Name your accounts and pay; the report is read from validated XRP Ledger mainnet and emailed to you, with every fact stamped with the ledger index it was read at.

ISSUER CONTROLS

For each issuing account: whether it kept the power to freeze holders, whether a freeze is on now, whether holding needs the issuer's permission, how many keys must sign, and supply concentration where it can be measured.

WHO CAN MOVE THE TREASURY

For each wallet, at your period end or today: balance, reserve and what is available, and how many keys it takes to move it. That count comes from summed signer weights, the master key and any regular key, not a headcount.

DEPTH THAT CAN ACTUALLY FILL

Quoted against funded depth within 10% of mid on the books you name, plus how much of the XRP held could be sold into the main books. AMM pools are shown separately, not added.

EVIDENCE ANYONE CAN RE-CHECK

Each report carries its findings in plain language, every ledger reply it was built from, and a SHA-256 over them. Your auditor can confirm the report on this site by that digest, and re-read every figure without trusting us.

How it is delivered

  • Emailed to the address you pay with, usually within minutes of payment: the report (open it in a browser, print to PDF) and the raw evidence file.
  • Quarterly and Audit support reports arrive by email the day after each period end, read at the last ledger of that day.
  • An invoice from Stripe with your billing address and tax ID.
  • If a reading cannot be taken, the report is not sent with a gap: it is retried, and if it still cannot be read you hear from us and are refunded for that report.

What it is not

A record of ledger facts at a stated ledger index, generated automatically. It is not an audit opinion, an attestation, a valuation, fair-value guidance under any accounting standard, or a legal or compliance determination about any asset or person. NOSHASHI never asks for keys, seeds, balances or customer data: everything in the report is public ledger state for the addresses you name.

Every figure comes from NOSHASHI's free public tools (certificate, treasury report, book health), and you can run them yourself. The report is for teams who want it assembled, delivered on schedule and verifiable for the file. See a live sample built from public accounts.
