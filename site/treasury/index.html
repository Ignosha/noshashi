Free · for CFOs and auditors of XRP treasuries

What did the treasury hold, who could move it, and what would the market have taken?

Enter the wallets and a period end. This reads validated XRP Ledger mainnet at the last ledger that closed that day. It reports each wallet's balance and reserve, who can move the funds, and how much of the XRP could have been sold into funded dollar offers. That is the depth a mark can actually lean on. Quoted depth is often much larger. Print it or save it as a PDF for the file.

WHAT IT ANSWERS

Who can move the holding: the master key, any regular key, and the signer list, counted by signer weight rather than by heads. And what the mark rests on: for the Bitstamp USD, GateHub USD and RLUSD books, how much XRP the funded offers within 10% of mid could have absorbed, and at what average price. AMM pools are shown beside each book but not added to it.

WHAT IT IS NOT

It is not a valuation, an audit opinion, or a statement of fair value under any accounting standard. It never asks for a key or a seed, and it stores nothing. Every figure carries its ledger index, and the raw ledger replies are one click away, so anyone can recompute the report without trusting us.
