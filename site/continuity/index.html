Ledger Desk · Continuity

Continuity

Check that one issued-token deposit was credited to your customers exactly once, for the right token and amount. Local checks stay on this device. Live verification sends the transaction hash to two public Mainnet servers and, when selected, to the read-only accounting adapter on this computer.

How Continuity works

  1. 01 / THE LEDGERRead the deposit twice. The transaction is fetched from two public Mainnet servers, s1 and s2.ripple.com, and their replies must match: validated, successful, and the same delivered amount, token and issuer.
  2. 02 / YOUR BOOKSRead what you credited. Posted credits come from your export or, with the open-source adapter, from a read-only SQLite view on your own machine, over loopback. They never leave the device.
  3. 03 / THE ANSWERExactly once, or why not. Right token from the right issuer, the amount actually delivered, one credit. A short delivery, a token from another issuer, a duplicate credit or a missing one is named, and the evidence can be exported.

Built for exchanges, payment firms and custodians crediting RLUSD, EUR or any issued token. XRP payments are refused with a reason.

Issued tokens only (RLUSD, EUR, any IOU); XRP payments are refused with a reason. Currency codes can be written as text (RLUSD) or as the ledger's 40-character hex. Amounts are decimal strings; exponent form (1.5e-7) is accepted.

For live reconciliation, supply credits with txHash, amount, issuer and currency, plus creditExportComplete (true only for a complete export). Optional workflows may contain a name and a dependencies list.

Ready to rehearse.

Run it on your own books

The rehearsal above checks the ledger side. To check real deposits against what your system actually posted, download Continuity and run it next to your accounting database. It reads your credits read-only, listens on 127.0.0.1 only, and sends nothing to NOSHASHI.
