EXPLAIN THE
DECISION.

Institutional asset operations need more than a transaction status. NOSHASHI is building toward a reproducible connection between proposed action, validated ledger evidence, human review context and subsequent investigation.

Development-stage proposal. The research gateway currently supports limited read-only XRPL transaction corroboration. The end-to-end institutional workflow, multi-tenant controls and enterprise service commitments described below remain proposed capabilities, not delivered production features.

Target lifecycle

One transaction. Three questions.

01 / BEFORE

Should we proceed?

Target: review destination and issuer constraints against the institution's current policy with an explicit HOLD on missing data.

02 / DURING

What changed?

Target: compare material ledger and control conditions with the original human approval snapshot.

03 / AFTER

What actually happened?

Prototype: corroborate validated XRP Ledger transaction outcome across two RPC sources; preserve reason codes and unknowns.

Institutional differentiation to validate

Why buy instead of build?

What established tools already do

XRPL explorers expose ledger data; custody platforms control assets; compliance suites monitor exposure; finance systems reconcile activity. Those are indispensable and often sophisticated.

What NOSHASHI aims to add

A portable case record tying the reviewer's internal policy context to sourced ledger facts and later changes, without requiring replacement of existing tools. This integration advantage has not yet been validated by paying customers.

Design partner brief

Prove the economics.

FOUR-WEEK PROPOSAL

Scoped evaluation

20–50 authorized historical exception cases and one existing operator workflow. Proposed $5,000 paid pilot, subject to buyer agreement.

MEASURE

Time and evidence

Compare investigation minutes, sourced-evidence completeness, false finality claims and unsupported outcomes with the current process.

DECISION

Stop or expand

No enterprise pricing claim until measured value, integration feasibility, security controls and a named budget owner support expansion.
