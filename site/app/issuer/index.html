Issuer analysis

Check which controls an issuer keeps over the tokens it issues.

New here? Three steps

  1. Paste an issuer address below, or press one of the examples (Bitstamp, GateHub, Ripple).
  2. Read the controls: can it freeze holders, claw back balances, require approval, charge a transfer fee?
  3. Check the evidence and the ledger it was read at, then note what you still need to verify yourself.

NOSHASHI shows what the ledger records about an issuer. It does not say whether a token is safe, legitimate or a good investment.

How this is worked out

  • Source: account_info with signer lists at the latest validated ledger, via /api/issuer-state.
  • Each control is read from the account's flags; nothing is inferred.
  • One passing check does not make a token safe. These are the issuer's powers, not a verdict on the issuer.

What was useful, confusing or missing? It goes privately to the founder; nothing is published. Only the first two fields are needed.
