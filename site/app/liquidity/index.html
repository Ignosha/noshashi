Liquidity analysis

Compare what an order book advertises with what could actually fill.

Pick a pair and measure it. You will see advertised depth, fillable depth on each side, and the spread, read at one validated ledger.

Details

How this is worked out

  • Source: book_offers both sides at one validated ledger, via /api/book (cached up to a minute at the edge).
  • Fillable counts only what each offer's owner held at that ledger (taker_gets_funded).
  • Within 10% of the reference mid; AMM pools are not included; up to 100 offers per side.

What was useful, confusing or missing? It goes privately to the founder; nothing is published. Only the first two fields are needed.

At least a few words. Never paste a seed phrase, private key or confidential trading information.
