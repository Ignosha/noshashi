Asset intelligence

See every token an account holds and which of its issuers can freeze or claw it back.

Example:

Paste an account address. You will get its token balances, the issuer of each, and whether that issuer can still freeze or claw the balance back.

Details

How this is worked out

  • Trust lines: account_lines at the latest validated ledger (first 200 lines).
  • Issuer controls: each issuer's account flags from /api/issuer-state, up to 12 issuers.
  • A line marked frozen means the issuer has frozen that specific line.
  • Read-only. No wallet is connected and nothing is signed.

Wrong result, confusing wording, missing feature or a data discrepancy? Tell us. It goes privately to the founder; nothing is published.

The page and the last input you analyzed are attached so we can reproduce it. Never send keys or seed phrases.
