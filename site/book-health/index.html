Book health: how much of the XRP Ledger's order books can actually fill?

An offer stays on the book at its full size after its owner has spent the money behind it. This page reads the main XRP books from validated ledger state and separates what is quoted from what is funded, for today and for the close of each of the last seven days. Nothing is stored: every day is re-read from the ledger at the index shown.

The last seven days

Share of the quoted ask that was funded, at the last ledger to close each day (UTC). The ask side is the depth an XRP holder sells into.

Book

HOW TO READ IT

Quoted is what the offers advertise within 10% of the book's reference mid. Funded is the part the accounts making those offers still held at that ledger. A low funded share means a counterparty's tooling sees far more depth than could trade. AMM pools are shown beside each book and are not added to it.

CHECK IT YOURSELF

Every reading names its ledger index. The JSON for today, or add ?at=YYYY-MM-DD for a past day. Each book's raw offers are at /api/book?…&format=raw. For a company's own holdings against these books, use the treasury report.
