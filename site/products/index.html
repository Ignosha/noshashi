NOSHASHI · products for the XRP Ledger

Products that read the ledger, not the label.

Every product reads validated XRP Ledger state, returns the ledger index it read and a SHA-256 receipt, and never holds keys, signs or moves funds. The research behind them is published under Research, and each product page says what it costs and what it does not do.

01 / FEATURED BUILD · DEPOSIT RECONCILIATION

Continuity

Check that one issued-token deposit was credited to your customers exactly once, for the right token and amount, read from two Mainnet servers against your own posted credits.

Free rehearsal · operator adapter open source →

Rippling Exposure API
