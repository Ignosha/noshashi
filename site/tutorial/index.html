Get started

  1. Install it. Download for macOS, Windows or Linux. The builds are not code-signed yet. On a Mac, right-click the app and choose Open the first time. On Windows, choose More info then Run anyway.
  2. Press Get started. The app connects to the XRP Ledger. The top bar reads ONLINE when it is live.
  3. Pick a view. Executive shows the eight key screens. Analyst shows every tool. Switch any time at the top of the sidebar.
  4. Jump anywhere. Press Ctrl/⌘+K and type a screen, a wallet or a command.

NOSHASHI only reads the ledger. It never asks for a private key or seed phrase, and it never signs anything.

Your first check

Find out whether a payment can go ahead, before anyone signs it.

  1. Open Verification in the sidebar.
  2. Paste the account you would pay into Destination account.
  3. Enter the amount in XRP, or tap 100, 1,000, 25,000 or 500,000.
  4. Pick a rule profile under Rule profile. Each one lists the credentials it requires.
  5. Press Run compliance gate.
GO

Every blocking rule passed.

HOLD

Blocking rules passed, but an advisory rule failed. Someone should sign it off.

NO-GO

A blocking rule failed. Do not send it.

Under the verdict, each rule shows Pass or Fail with the reason. The cryptographic receipt (SHA-256) proves which facts and rules produced the result. Every run is kept in the Session log.

Compliance

Can this payment or asset move under your rules?

Verification

Free

GO, HOLD or NO-GO for a payment, before anyone signs it.

  1. Paste the destination account and enter an amount (or tap 100, 1,000, 25,000 or 500,000).
  2. Pick a rule profile, for example US_REGULATED_DEX.
  3. Press Run compliance gate (Ctrl/⌘+Enter). Read the verdict, each rule's pass or fail, and the receipt.

Ledger Watch

Enterprise

Screen deposits before you credit them.

  1. Add your deposit addresses, each with a label.
  2. Every incoming payment is judged: what really arrived, whether the token is genuine, phishing dust, and who funded the sender up to three hops back.
  3. Receive the results as signed webhooks and a feed.

Credentials

Free

Which on-ledger credentials (XLS-70) an account holds.

  1. Paste a counterparty address, and the issuer's if you know it.
  2. Press Read.
  3. See each credential, who issued it and whether it is still valid. Switch between Grid and Table.

Domain Grid

Free

Every permissioned domain (XLS-80) on mainnet, and who qualifies.

  1. Browse the list, or paste a domain ID or owner address and press Read live.
  2. Open a domain to see the credentials it requires.
  3. Paste a counterparty and press Check to see whether it would be admitted.

Provenance

Pro

How old an account is and who funded it.

  1. Paste the account (r…).
  2. Read when it was created and who sent its first XRP.
  3. Follow the funding chain as far as the node can prove it.

Authority

Pro

What an asset's issuer can still do to it.

  1. Paste the issuer (r…).
  2. Read freeze, clawback and signing powers, and how concentrated the holdings are.
  3. Keep the digest: anyone can re-check the same reading later.

Security Center

Free

Protect an account, check who you are paying, and recover from a theft.

  1. Pick a tool from the tabs along the top. Start with Account check: paste your own address and press Check.
  2. Read the grade and each finding in plain English. Fixes come as unsigned transactions you copy and sign in your own wallet.
  3. Before paying someone, use Pre-sign check or Safe send. If an account was drained, use Recover funds or Incident response. Tabs that need a paid plan say so.

Liquidity

Is there real depth to buy or sell?

Exposure Analysis

Pro

Freeze rights, Travel Rule scope and counterparty concentration.

  1. Load a wallet.
  2. See which issuers could freeze what it holds.
  3. See which payments fall under the Travel Rule, and where exposure is concentrated.

Order Book

Pro

How much of the quoted depth can actually fill.

  1. Enter a currency code (for example USD) and its issuer.
  2. Compare quoted against can fill on each side.
  3. Offers whose owners no longer hold the asset are not counted.

Pool Governance

Pro

Who controls an AMM pool's trading fee.

  1. Paste the AMM's account (r…).
  2. See each fee vote and the share of liquidity behind it.
  3. See who holds the auction slot, and its discount, right now.

Portfolio & Radar

Pro

Many wallets, one rule set, failures first.

  1. Add wallets with a label, for example Treasury · hot wallet.
  2. Each one is checked against the same rules.
  3. Failures are listed first, so you know where to look.

Evidence

Can you prove what happened?

Audit Trail

Free

An account's validated history, with what each payment really delivered.

  1. Paste the account and press Read history. Set the Flag at or above amount if you need one.
  2. Filter by All, In, Out or Flagged, or search by hash, tag or memo.
  3. Press Export evidence for a file with a SHA-256 manifest.

Ledger & Policy

Pro

Every verdict you have run, your policy, and a signed export.

  1. Write or activate your institution's policy.
  2. Every verdict is kept on this device with the rules that produced it.
  3. Export a signed record an examiner can verify.

Settlement

Pro

What a transaction actually delivered, not what it asked for.

  1. Paste the transaction hash (64 characters).
  2. Compare the requested amount with the delivered amount.
  3. Partial payments are called out, so you never credit the wrong figure.

Questions

Do I need a wallet, a key or an account?

No. The app reads public ledger data. It never asks for a private key or seed phrase and never signs. An account is only needed to carry a paid plan, portfolios or API keys.

What does a screen marked Pro, Institutional or Enterprise do if I am on Free?

It shows what it does and offers Sign in or See plans. Nothing is hidden behind a fake preview. Prices are on the pricing page.

The top bar says DEGRADED or RECONNECTING.

DEGRADED means one public node is behind the others. It usually clears within a few ledgers. RECONNECTING means the link dropped, and nothing is shown as live until it is back. Press Ctrl/⌘+R to re-read.

Where is my data kept?

Verdicts, policies and settings stay on your device. The Trust & Security screen lists exactly what is read and where anything goes.

I am stuck.

Open NOSHX and use the Support tab, or contact us. The Learn screen has hands-on labs for each part of the app.
