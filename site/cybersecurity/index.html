The website lab is local arithmetic. The desktop suite reads public XRPL data. Neither holds keys, signs transactions or moves funds.

Evidence can help a response. It cannot reverse XRP.

Validated XRP payments have no chargeback, and XRP cannot be frozen or clawed back by a token issuer. A verified custodian may investigate a deposit under its procedures. An issued-token issuer may have conditional freeze or clawback powers over its own tokens. NOSHASHI cannot seize assets, guarantee recovery, identify an attacker from a graph alone or secure a compromised device.

Never share a seed, private key or recovery phrase. Preserve hashes and timestamps; verify a service’s official contact route independently. Destination tags and self-declared domains do not verify a custodian.

TREASURY GUARD / DEVICE-LOCAL BETA

A large transfer deserves a human check.

Set XRP limits for individual payments, a rolling window and the share of the account balance. Flag destinations outside your allowlist and large totals split across several wallets. Inspect the validated payment evidence, then mark the activity expected or not recognized.

01 / BEFORE SIGNING

Review the proposal.

Check an unsigned direct XRP Payment against saved static limits. This is advisory: current balance, cumulative activity, destination risk and independent approvals need separate checks. Your signing system must enforce any hold.

02 / AFTER VALIDATION

Notify the configured operator.

Local device notifications while the app is open, with permission. Reads run approximately once a minute, subject to node availability and backlog. No wallet owner is inferred or automatically emailed.

03 / RESPONSE

Preserve evidence. Investigate.

Record a local review, export the complete local record with a hash and open incident or signing-control tools. A threshold flag is not proof of theft; validated XRP cannot be reversed.

Free: three local watches. Pro and above: fifty. A new watch begins at its first successful ledger baseline. Direct XRP Payments require delivered-amount and sender-balance evidence; unsupported payment forms and incomplete data remain review gaps. Monitoring stops when the app is closed. Hosted payment alerts, verified recipients, enforced approvals and response commitments require a separately configured integration; they are not supplied by this local feature.

Beta boundaries, beside the tools.

Public-node responses do not independently verify consensus, legal rights, reserves or compliance. SHA-256 hashes identify evidence bytes; they are not digital signatures. A local reference is editable. The desktop installers are not OS code-signed or notarized; updater signatures are separate.

SSO, dedicated environments and uptime SLAs are planned, not offered here. No independent security assessment, certification or guaranteed recovery service is claimed.
