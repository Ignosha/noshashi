Product brief · beta

NOSHASHI: proof for every decision made on the XRP Ledger.

NOSHASHI is a read-only compliance and risk layer for the XRP Ledger. Before money moves it answers three questions: can this move under our policy, is there enough real liquidity to exit at the price we mark, and can we prove later why we said yes. It reads validated ledger state, never holds keys and never signs anything.

01

How it solves XRPL's problems

Each of these is a property of the ledger itself, not a bug in it. They become problems when a wallet, a spreadsheet or a compliance file reads the ledger the easy way.

The problemWhat NOSHASHI doesWhere
Holders cannot see what an issuer can still do to a tokenAn issuer can keep the power to freeze a balance, claw it back, or require its approval to hold the token at all. A wallet shows none of this. Reads the issuer's own account flags and signer list from validated ledger state and states each power plainly, with the ledger index it was read at and a digest anyone can recompute.Authority certificate Issuer badge Issuer alerts AUTHORITY EXPOSURE ANALYSIS
Order books advertise depth that nobody can fillAn offer stays on the book at its full size after its owner has spent the money behind it. Depth summed from the advertised sizes can be many times what would actually trade. Counts only what each offer's owner still holds (taker_gets_funded), within a stated band around the mid, and shows quoted and fillable side by side.ORDER BOOK Treasury report EXPOSURE ANALYSIS
A payment can deliver far less than it saysA partial payment can state 1,000,000 USD and deliver 0.000001 USD. Tools that record the stated amount record money that never arrived. Reads the delivered amount the ledger recorded, flags partial payments, and screens deposits on what arrived before they are credited.SETTLEMENT LEDGER WATCH Delivered-amount export
Counterfeit tokens, phishing dust and lookalike addressesAnyone can issue a token called USD. A drop of dust can carry a link. An address can be made to match the first and last characters of a real one. Flags a familiar ticker whose issuer owes nothing, dust carrying a link, lookalike addresses, and senders funded by a sanctioned account up to three hops back.LEDGER WATCH CHECK AN ADDRESS INBOX SECURITY CENTER PROVENANCE
Who can move an account is easy to misreadA signer list is a weighted vote, not a headcount. A regular key signs alone. An account with no usable key cannot move at all. Lists every route to a signature (master key, regular key, signer list counted by weight) and leads with the weakest, which is the one an auditor has to rely on.CONTROL SURFACE Treasury report
A compliance check is a private log nobody else can verifyWhen a firm says it checked a counterparty, there is usually no way to prove the check ran, on what data, or under which rules. Every verdict is GO, HOLD or NO-GO with the rule that decided it, sealed in a SHA-256 receipt over the ledger index, the rules and the policy version, and written to an append-only audit log.VERIFICATION LEDGER & POLICY AUDIT TRAIL
One public server can be wrongPublic XRPL servers lag, rate-limit and occasionally disagree. A reading from one server is only as good as that server. Reads only validated ledgers, compares several public servers, and issues no GO when the sources it needs do not agree.LEDGER SYNC MISSION CONTROL
New compliance primitives are hard to readXLS-70 credentials and XLS-80 permissioned domains decide who may transact, but their state lives in raw ledger objects. Resolves credential acceptance, expiry and revocation from ledger flags, and shows what each domain requires and how far a wallet is from meeting it.CREDENTIALS DOMAIN GRID
Regulatory scoping is done by handTravel Rule thresholds and counterparty concentration are worked out in spreadsheets, after the fact. Marks which settlements cross the Travel Rule (FATF R.16) threshold in a chosen jurisdiction and lack counterparty data, and scores concentration with the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index.EXPOSURE ANALYSIS PORTFOLIO & RADAR
