IV.

Security and controls

Built for the people who sign off on it: compliance, risk and audit.

  • Read-only by designNOSHASHI never holds keys and never signs. There is nothing to connect and nothing to move.
  • Validated state onlyEvery reading comes from a validated ledger and names its index.
  • ReceiptsA SHA-256 digest over every answer, so anyone can confirm what was read.
  • Versioned policyGO, HOLD or NO-GO against a policy your team versions and reviews.
  • Audit logWho checked what, when, and what the ledger said at the time.
  • Open sourceThe code is public on GitHub, for your own review.

VII.

A note from the founder

Institutions looking at the XRP Ledger are asked to take a great deal on trust: that an issuer will not freeze a balance, that a quoted book is really there, that a payment delivered what it said. The ledger already holds the answers. NOSHASHI reads them, names the ledger they came from, and keeps the receipt.

We are a young company and we say so plainly. There are no borrowed logos here, only readings you can check for yourself.
