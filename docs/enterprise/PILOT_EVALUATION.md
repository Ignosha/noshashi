# Proposed institutional evaluation — commercial planning only

Proposed package: **$5,000 scoped evaluation**, subject to founder agreement, legal/security review and the [release gate](RELEASE_GATE.md). This document does not create a Stripe price, contract, purchase, customer commitment, guarantee or paid entitlement.

Suggested bounded scope: one named institution, one defined workflow, agreed sample set and evaluation period; read-only XRPL evidence; public or explicitly permitted synthetic/recorded inputs; browser demonstrations first. Payment verification, issuer authority and funded-versus-quoted liquidity are separate questions. No keys, fund transfers, production execution, compliance certification, bespoke future feature or guaranteed ROI is included.

| Success criterion (agree before work) | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|
| Explain whether an invoice was fully delivered | Requested/delivered amounts, destination/tag, validation, source/time, explicit shortfall/unknown outcome |
| Explain issuer permissions and limits | Verified public settings with off-ledger questions clearly separated |
| Interpret exit-depth limitations | Same-snapshot quoted/funded depth, units, direction, price band and freshness |
| Support a reviewer decision | Versioned inputs/policy, named reviewer, traceable canonical evidence and limitations |
| Respect institutional access boundaries | Dedicated test identities, cross-tenant denial, no Pro-to-Institutional escalation, revocation checks |

Onboarding checklist: named sponsor/evaluator and security contact; permitted data/use; agreed product/version and platform; approved access scope/contract; support/escalation owner and hours; retention/export rules; known limitations acknowledged; success criteria signed off; no reliance on unverified production controls.

Deliverables may include an evaluation report, source-backed sample cases, workflow walkthrough, evidence exports/digests, observed errors and a prioritized fit/gap review. They are proposals until agreed. Browser/OS support is limited to actual test evidence; physical Mac deployment requires its own release/device acceptance.

Completion report template: organization/evaluator (private), dates/version/scope, test cases and source timestamps, success criteria met/not met, failures/limitations, data/export disposition, actual observed benefit (do not infer savings), evaluator feedback, agreed next step and founder acceptance. Pricing negotiations, legal/security assertions and expansion commitments go to the founder.

Current launch decision: NO-GO for a paid enterprise pilot until the scoped release/security/access and operational acceptance gates are satisfied. Public read-only demonstrations remain available; their availability is not pilot acceptance.
