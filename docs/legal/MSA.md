## 1. Definitions

- **Service:** the NOSHASHI software and hosted features Customer
  subscribes to under an Order Form, including:
  - the desktop application
  - the hosted organization features (shared policies, exceptions,
    investigations, audit log)
  - the Compliance API
  - webhooks and event feeds
  - the Documentation
- **Order Form:** a document referencing this Agreement, signed by both
  parties, that states the plan, term, fees and limits.
- **Customer Data:** data Customer or its Users submit to the hosted
  parts of the Service. It includes policies, exceptions, investigation
  notes, organization membership and audit records, but not public
  ledger data.
- **Public Ledger Data:** data read from the public XRP Ledger. It is
  public, and neither party owns it through this Agreement.
- **User:** an individual Customer authorizes to use the Service under
  their own account.
- **Documentation:** NOSHASHI's published documentation for the Service,
  as updated from time to time.
- **Verdict:** a GO, HOLD or NO-GO result, and its receipt, produced by
  the Service's rule engine.

## 2. The Service

2.1 **Access.** During the subscription term, NOSHASHI grants Customer
a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Service for its
internal business purposes, within the limits in the Order Form and the
Documentation.

2.2 **What the Service is.** The Service reads public ledger state and
evaluates it against rules Customer configures. It does not:
- hold, custody, sign, submit or move any asset
- ask for or store any private key or seed
- execute transactions

2.3 **Not advice.** Verdicts, reports and assistant output are
information, not legal, regulatory, tax, accounting or investment
advice. They are not a determination that any person has acted
lawfully or unlawfully. Customer remains responsible for its own
compliance decisions, and for review by its qualified personnel.

2.4 **Changes.** NOSHASHI may improve the Service. It will not
materially reduce the core functionality of a plan Customer has
subscribed to during the current term.

2.5 **Features not yet available.** A feature marked "coming soon" or
"not yet available" on NOSHASHI's pricing page is not part of the
Service unless an Order Form states that it is included, with a
delivery date.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

3.1 Customer is responsible for:
- its Users' compliance with this Agreement
- keeping account credentials confidential
- enabling two-factor authentication where offered
- the accuracy of the rules and thresholds it configures

3.2 Customer will not:
- use the Service in breach of the Acceptable Use, Sanctions and Export
  terms published at `/legal/`
- reverse-engineer the hosted Service
- probe or test its security without NOSHASHI's prior written consent
- share API keys between organizations
- use the Service to build a competing product

4.1 **Fees.** Customer pays the fees in the Order Form. Unless the

- payment is due within thirty (30) days of the invoice date
- payment is by ACH, wire or another method the Order Form names

4.2 **Taxes.** Fees exclude taxes. Customer pays applicable sales, use,
VAT and similar taxes, other than taxes on NOSHASHI's income.

4.3 **Late payment.** If an undisputed invoice is more than thirty
(30) days overdue, NOSHASHI may suspend the hosted Service after giving
ten (10) days' written notice.

4.4 **Renewal and price changes.** An Order Form renews for successive
periods equal to its initial term unless either party gives notice of
non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the term ends. NOSHASHI
will give at least sixty (60) days' notice of any price change for a
renewal term.

## 5. Customer Data and privacy

5.1 **Ownership.** Customer owns Customer Data. NOSHASHI uses Customer
Data only to provide and support the Service, to keep it secure, and as
the law requires.

5.2 **Personal data.** Where NOSHASHI processes personal data on
Customer's behalf, the Data Processing Addendum (**DPA**) applies.

5.3 **Security.** NOSHASHI maintains the technical and organizational
measures described in the DPA, Annex 2.

5.4 **Audit records.** Organization audit-log records, including
sign-in records, are append-only by design. They are kept for the life
of the organization, and are provided to Customer on request at the end
of the term.

5.5 **Return and deletion.** Within thirty (30) days after the
Agreement ends, and on Customer's written request, NOSHASHI will export
Customer Data in a machine-readable format. It will then delete it from
the live system. Encrypted backups expire within ninety (90) days.
