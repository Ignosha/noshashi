# Continuity RLUSD roadmap

RLUSD on XRPL Mainnet is the initial commercial focus. XRP payment review remains available. The first institutional outcome is a traceable explanation of which payment conditions were observed, which changed between reviews, and what remains unknown before a payment proceeds through the institution’s wallet workflow.

| --- | --- |
| Asset identity | The approved application identity is XRPL Mainnet, issuer `rMxCKbEDwqr76QuheSUMdEGf4B9xJ8m5De`, and wire currency `524C555344000000000000000000000000000000`. A symbol or matching currency under another issuer is insufficient. The registry entry comes from Ripple’s published token details. [Ripple token addresses](https://docs.ripple.com/products/stablecoin/overview/token-addresses) · [RLUSD on XRPL](https://docs.ripple.com/products/stablecoin/developer-resources/rlusd-on-the-xrpl) |
| Holder-to-holder preflight | The app pins the issuer and both holder lines to one validated XRPL ledger hash and index. It checks directional freeze, deep freeze, issuer authorization requirements and destination-tag observations. Missing, stale or misattributed evidence cannot establish clearance. Issuer-directed payments, route selection, balances, transfer rate, DepositAuth, and destination line capacity remain unevaluated. Clear observations still produce HOLD. [AccountRoot](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/protocol/ledger-data/ledger-entry-types/accountroot) · [RippleState](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/protocol/ledger-data/ledger-entry-types/ripplestate) · [Freezing tokens](https://xrpl.org/docs/concepts/tokens/fungible-tokens/freezes) |
| Payment-condition comparison | The local comparator compares complete observations at distinct validated ledgers for the same request. It returns `CHANGED`, `UNCHANGED`, or `UNKNOWN` with ledger anchors and changed flags. `UNCHANGED` applies to the two sampled observations. The local reader can fail over across public RPC endpoints. Independent agreement and background monitoring remain open. |
| Supply observations | The separate RLUSD intelligence endpoint implements XRPL issuer obligations and Ethereum contract `totalSupply` as independent metrics with method, source and chain anchors. XRPL obligations omit some categories, and Ethereum contract supply includes more than circulating balances. The endpoint preserves missing values and does not sum the two metrics. ## Delivery order

| Priority | Increment | Acceptance criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Demo | Show exact RLUSD identity, a historical or explicitly simulated change, and the evidence boundary. | Point to ledger anchors, explain a changed condition, and reproduce the same offline result without requiring a wallet, private key or network write. |
| Pilot P0 | Independently corroborate holder and issuer reads, define observation age, and bind observations to an institutional review. | Provider disagreement, an expired review, missing ledger history or partial evidence each return a typed UNKNOWN or HOLD. Acceptance includes read-only outage and restart tests and a retained receipt bound to asset, source, destination, ledger, policy and actors. |
| Pilot P0 | Expand direct-payment readiness and post-transaction verification. | Check validated destination policy, deposit authorization, holder-line capacity, available balance, transfer rate and fee headroom for supported paths. Separately verify transaction finality, actual delivered amount and destination. Path payments and assets beyond the supported model stay unknown. |
| Pilot P1 | Record condition history and send review alerts under an explicit pilot scope. | Persist tenant-scoped observations with the ledger and assessment time. Deduplicate repeated conditions, mark expiry, and distinguish provider disagreement from issuer changes. Stakeholders exercise acknowledgement, suppression, false positives and replay. |
| Pilot P1 | Add dated holder concentration, counterparty exposures, protocol events and liquidity observations. | Show sample coverage, exclusions, direction, network, time and receipt links. Report insufficient coverage explicitly. Liquidity uses observed depth with methodology and amount, and cannot be inferred from issuance. |
| Evaluation | Observe market-price deviation from documented USD markets and track published reserve disclosures. | Record venue, pair, timestamps, data gaps and calculation. Test stale, thin and conflicting markets before choosing alert thresholds. Preserve reserve-report publisher, reporting period, publication date and source reference separately from on-chain observations. [Ripple transparency reports](https://ripple.com/products/stablecoin/transparency/) |
| Evaluation | Expand the adapter contract beyond XRPL. | Keep asset identity network-specific. Evaluate Ethereum Mainnet using chain ID, canonical proxy and block anchors, actual token transfer logs and applicable token permissions. XRPL trust lines and destination tags remain XRPL-specific. [Supported networks](https://docs.ripple.com/products/stablecoin/overview/supported-chains-and-networks) |

Ripple publishes reserve reports that include third-party attestations concerning management’s assertions for the stated period. NOSHASHI can track those reports and record their provenance. Independent reserve verification, continuous reserve sufficiency and execution guarantees remain outside the scope of these observations. [Ripple transparency reports](https://ripple.com/products/stablecoin/transparency/)
