**Title:** XRP Ledger developer: token checks, payments and dashboards (TypeScript, Rust)

**Overview:**

> I build software on the XRP Ledger. My own product, NOSHASHI, is a
> read-only risk tool for XRPL that I designed and shipped end to end:
> a desktop app for macOS, Windows and Linux (Tauri, React, Rust), a
> public website with live tools that read mainnet, a Supabase backend
> with row-level security, and Stripe billing. The source is public, so
> you can read my code before hiring me:
> https://github.com/Ignosha/noshashi
>
> What I can do for you on XRPL:
> - Check a token before you list, hold or accept it: issuer freeze,
>   clawback and authorization settings, holders, trust lines.
> - Payment handling that reads `delivered_amount`, so partial payments
>   can't credit more than arrived.
> - Order-book and AMM reads: how much of a book can actually fill.
> - Dashboards and reports from validated ledgers, with a SHA-256 record
>   anyone can re-check.
> - Integrations with xrpl.js, wallets, webhooks and Supabase or
>   Postgres backends.
>
> I work remotely, write plain status updates, and only claim what I
> can show running.
